# AFS – Aesthetic Fluency Scale

## Overview

This folder contains files related to AFS – Aesthetic Fluency Scale. This scale measures self-reported knowledge about the arts and was first developed and reported by Smith and Smith (2006). Some domain-specific versions of the scale (e.g., film) have been developed. A revised version of the scale was developed and reported by Cotter et al. (2023).

## Administration and Scoring

The original scale (marked AFS below) has 10 items. It is included below for historical interest. Psychometric details of the original scale can be found here:

Cotter, K. N., Chen, D. F., Christensen, A. P., Kim, K. Y., \& Silvia, P. J. (2023). Measuring art knowledge: Item response theory and differential item functioning analysis of the Aesthetic Fluency Scale. Psychology of Aesthetics, Creativity, and the Arts, 17(3), 358–368. https://doi.org/10.1037/aca0000397

Smith, L. F., \& Smith, J. K. (2006). The nature and growth of aesthetic fluency. In P. Locher, C. Martindale, \& L. Dorfman (Eds.), New directions in aesthetics, creativity, and the arts (pp. 47-58). Amityville, NY: Baywood.

The revised scale (marked AFS-R below) is the recommended version for research use. It has a longer form (36 items) as well as a shorter form (10 items). The revised scale was reported and developed here:

Cotter, K. N., Rodriguez-Boerwinkle, R. M., Christensen, A. P., Fekete, A., Smith, J. K., Smith, L. F., ... \& Silvia, P. J. (2023). Updating the Aesthetic Fluency Scale: Revised long and short forms for research in the psychology of the arts. PLOS One, 18(2), e0281547. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0281547
