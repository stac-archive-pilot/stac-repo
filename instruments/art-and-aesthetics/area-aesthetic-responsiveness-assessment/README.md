# AReA – Aesthetic Responsiveness Assessment

## Overview

This folder contains files related to AReA – Aesthetic Responsiveness Assessment. This scale was reported and developed by Schlotz et al. (2021). The archive has English, German, and Farsi versions.

## Files

* **AReA\_English\_German.docx**
* **AReA\_English\_German.pdf**
* **AReA\_English\_Qualtrics.qsf**
* **AReA\_Farsi\_English.docx**
* **AReA\_Farsi\_English.pdf**

## Administration and Scoring

The AReA scale has three subscales measuring different aspects of aesthetic responsiveness. 

Scale users should note that the original article has a scoring method where 1 item is included in 2 subscales, and this scoring approach is reflected in the files below:

Schlotz, W., Wallot, S., Omigie, D., Masucci, M. D., Hoelzmann, S. C., \& Vessel, E. A. (2021). The Aesthetic Responsiveness Assessment (AReA): A screening tool to assess individual differences in responsiveness to art in English and German. Psychology of Aesthetics, Creativity, and the Arts, 15(4), 682–696. https://doi.org/10.1037/aca0000348

Other psychometric research on the AReA scale suggests that the item can be assigned to one subscale exclusively:

Silvia, P. J., Rodriguez-Boerwinkle, R. M., Awa, K. N., Zabelina, D. L., \& Christensen, A. P. (2025). Evaluating the structure of the Aesthetic Responsiveness Assessment (AReA) with bootstrap exploratory graph analysis. Empirical Studies of the Arts, 43(1), 550-564.
