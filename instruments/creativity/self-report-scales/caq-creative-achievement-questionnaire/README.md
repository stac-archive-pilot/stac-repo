# CAQ – Creative Achievement Questionnaire

## Overview

This folder contains files related to CAQ – Creative Achievement Questionnaire.



The CAQ is one of the most popular measures of eminent creative achievements. It has been extensively used and translated into several languages. Researchers interested in measuring creative achievements should also consider the ICAA, included elsewhere in this archive.

## Administration and Scoring

The CAQ uses an uncommon scoring method, with a mix of binary items and multiplicative items. See the Carson et al. (2005) reference below for details on scoring. Researchers could consult these additional psychometric articles and reviews about the CAQ:

Silvia, P. J., Wigert, B., Reiter-Palmon, R., \& Kaufman, J. C. (2012). Assessing creativity with self-report scales: A review and empirical evaluation. Psychology of Aesthetics, Creativity, and the Arts, 6(1), 19–34. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0024071

Wang, C. C., Ho, H. C., Cheng, C. L., \& Cheng, Y. Y. (2014). Application of the Rasch model to the measurement of creativity: The creative achievement questionnaire. Creativity Research Journal, 26(1), 62-71.
