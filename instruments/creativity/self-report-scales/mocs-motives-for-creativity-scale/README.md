# MoCS - Motives for creativity scale

## Overview 
This folder contains materials related to the Motives for Creativity Scale (MoCS), a questionnaire measuring motives for engaging in creative behavior, each assessed with two items.

The original MoCS included nine motives (Benedek et al., 2020).
A tenth motive (passion) was introduced in later work (Benedek et al., 2025) and was found to be particularly relevant among creative professionals.

Items are available in English and German.

## Citation 
If you use this measure in your published work, please cite the reference(s) below.

Original scale:
Benedek, M., Bruckdorfer, R., & Jauk, E. (2020). Motives for creativity: Exploring the what and why of everyday creativity. Journal of Creative Behavior, 54(3), 610-625. https://doi.org/10.1002/jocb.396

Extension including the passion motive:
Benedek, M., Calisgan, G. Y., & Saretzki, J. (2025). How do creative professionals stand out? Comparing the creative potential of musicians and actors to non-artists. Personality and Individual Differences, 236, 112993. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.paid.2024.112993
