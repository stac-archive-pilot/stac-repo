# ICAA – Inventory of Creative Activities and Achievements

## Overview

This folder contains files related to ICAA – Inventory of Creative Activities and Achievements. The ICAA was first developed and published by Diedrich et al. (2018), and it is a popular measure of creative achievements and engagement in creative activities. The ICAA is available in English, French, German, and Japanese. A short form of the German version is available.
