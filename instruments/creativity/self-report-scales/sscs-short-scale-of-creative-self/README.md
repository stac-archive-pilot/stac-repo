# SSCS – Short Scale of Creative Self

## Overview

This folder contains files related to SSCS – Short Scale of Creative Self. This scale is widely-used in creativity research and was first published by Karwowski et al. (2018). See below for additional references to consult regarding administration and scoring. Researchers interested in creative self-efficacy could consult the CSES (Creative Self-Efficacy Scale) elsewhere in the STAC archive.

## Files

* **SSCS\_English.doc**
* **SSCS\_English.pdf**
* **SSCS\_English\_Qualtrics.qsf**

## Administration and Scoring

This scale has two subscales: creative self-efficacy and creative personal identity. Many researchers have used a 7-point response scale; many others have used a 5-point response scale. Users of the scale should consult a recent large scale item-response analysis, which offers psychometric details about the scale and suggests some merit to the 5-point scale over 7-point scale:

Zielińska, A., Lebuda, I., \& Karwowski, M. (2022). Scaling the creative self: An item response theory analysis of the short scale of creative self. Creativity Research Journal, 34(4), 431-444.
