# HEISS – Humor Efficacy and Identity Short Scales

## Overview

This folder contains files related to HEISS – Humor Efficacy and Identity Short Scales. These scales are grounded in Karwowski's research on creative self-concepts, which distinguishes between creative self-efficacy and creative personal identity. The HEISS measures these beliefs in the specific domain of humor via humor efficacy ("I can" beliefs related to being funny and making others laugh) and humor identity ("I am" beliefs related to the centrality of being funny to one's self-concept).

This archive contains versions in English, Polish, and German. A Traditional Chinese version was recently developed by Chiang (2024) in a Master's thesis ("A Reliability and Validity Study on the Development of the Traditional Chinese Version Humor Efficacy and Identity Short Scales (HEISS-TC)").

## Files

## Administration and Scoring

See the articles below for details about administration and scoring. Because all the items are scored in one direction, the original developers recommend the use of a reversed "trap item" to aid in catching straightlining and other forms of careless and inattentive responding.
