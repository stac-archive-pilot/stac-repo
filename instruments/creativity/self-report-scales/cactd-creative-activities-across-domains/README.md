# CActD - Creative Activities across Domains

## Overview 
This folder contains files related to the Creative Activities across domains (CActD) scale.
It asks for the frequency of creative engagement in nine creative domains over the last year.

## Files
- **CActD_English.docx:** English version
- **CActD_German.docx:** German version

## Administration and Scoring
Self-report scale. The time range can be adjusted if needed.
Consider activity scores per domain and/or averaged across all nine domains.

## Citation 
If you use this measure in your published work, please consider citing the reference(s) below:

The article that first used it, see Supplemental material Table S2 in Benedek et al., 2020:
Benedek, M., Bruckdorfer, R., & Jauk, E. (2020). Motives for creativity: Exploring the what and why of everyday creativity. Journal of Creative Behavior, 54(3), 610-625. https://doi.org/10.1002/jocb.396

The article that examined validity in a Little-c and Pro-c context:
Benedek, M., Calisgan, G. Y., & Saretzki, J. (2025). How do creative professionals stand out? Comparing the creative potential of musicians and actors to non-artists. Personality and Individual Differences, 236, 112993. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.paid.2024.112993
