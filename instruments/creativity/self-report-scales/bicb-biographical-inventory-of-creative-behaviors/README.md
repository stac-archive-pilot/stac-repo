# BICB – Biographical Inventory of Creative Behaviors

## Overview

This folder contains files related to BICB – Biographical Inventory of Creative Behaviors. This scale was developed by Batey (2007), and it has emerged as a popular scale for measuring engagement in everyday creative behaviors. See below for additional details.

## Administration and Scoring

The scale was reported in Batey's dissertation:

Batey, M. (2007). A psychometric investigation of everyday creativity. Unpublished Doctoral Thesis. University College, London.

Researchers interested in using this scale can find psychometric evaluations and discussions in the following articles:

Silvia, P. J., Rodriguez, R. M., Beaty, R. E., Frith, E., Kaufman, J. C., Loprinzi, P., \& Reiter-Palmon, R. (2021). Measuring everyday creativity: A Rasch model analysis of the Biographical Inventory of Creative Behaviors (BICB) scale. Thinking Skills and Creativity, 39, 100797.

Silvia, P. J., Wigert, B., Reiter-Palmon, R., \& Kaufman, J. C. (2012). Assessing creativity with self-report scales: A review and empirical evaluation. Psychology of Aesthetics, Creativity, and the Arts, 6(1), 19–34. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0024071
