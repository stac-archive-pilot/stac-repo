# TopCA - Top Creative Achievements

## Overview 
This folder contains files related to the assessment of top creative achievements

## Files
- **Top3CA_English.docx:** English version of top-3 creative achievement measure
- **Top3CA_German.docx:** German version of top-3 creative achievement measure

## Administration and Scoring
People are asked to report their k (e.g. 3) most notable creative achievements.
The reported are rated on a 7-point scale by humans raters and/or LLMs.

## Citation 
If you use this measure in your published work, please cite the reference(s) below: 

Meinzer, N. Saretzki, J., Ceh, S. M., & Benedek, M. (in press). Automated scoring of creative achievements. Journal of Creative Behavior.
