# SCTT - he scientific creative thinking test

## Overview 
This folder contains files related to SCTT - he scientific creative thinking test. The SCTT assesses different aspects of scientific creativity, focusing on generating research questions, hypothesis generation, and research design.

## Files
- SCTT template - a Word template of the SCTT
- SCTT.qsf - a Qualtrics version of the SCTT
- Beaty et al., 2024.pdf - the paper presenting SCTT

## Administration and Scoring
Administration and scoring is described in Beaty et al., 2024. Additional information can be found at: https://osf.io/439zs/overview?view_only=4cbda208526948a99afba0050a2c043f

Automatic scoring of the task is achieved via: https://cap.ist.psu.edu/sctt-ai

## Citation 

Beaty, R., Cortes, R. A., Luchini, S., Patterson, J. D., Forthmann, B., Baker, B. S., Barbot, B., Hardiman, M., & Green, A. (2024). The scientific creative thinking test (SCTT): Reliability, validity, and automated scoring. PsyArxiv Preprint.
