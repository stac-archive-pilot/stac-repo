# SRIS – Self-Reflection and Insight Scale

## Overview

This folder contains files related to SRIS – Self-Reflection and Insight Scale. This scale was developed by Grant et al. (2002) as part of the tradition on research on individual differences in self-awareness and private self-consciousness. The original scale and a short form are provided.

## Files

* **SRIS\_English.doc**
* **SRIS\_English.docx**
* **SRIS\_English.pdf**
* **SRIS\_Short Form\_English.pdf**

## Administration and Scoring

The original scale contains two subscales: self-reflection and insight.

Grant, A. M., Franklin, J., \& Langford, P. (2002). The Self-reflection and Insight Scale: A new measure of private self-consciousness. Social Behavior and Personality, 30, 821-836.

A 12-item short form was recently developed that provides psychometric details on both the original and short versions of the scale:

Silvia, P. J. (2022). The self-reflection and insight scale: Applying item response theory to craft an efficient short form. Current Psychology, 41(12), 8635-8645.
