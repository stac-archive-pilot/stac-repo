# Epistemic Curiosity Inventory

## Overview 
This folder contains files related to Epistemic Curiosity Inventory. This inventory measures epistemic curiosity.

## Files
- Epistemic_curiosity.qsf: a Qualtrics implementation.
- Litman et al., 2003.pdf: the paper presenting the inventory

## Administration and Scoring
Scoring is described in the paper presenting the inventory.

## Citation 
If you use this measure in your published work, please cite the reference(s) below: 

Litman, J. A., & Spielberger, C. D. (2003). Measuring epistemic curiosity and its diversive and specific components. Journal of personality assessment, 80(1), 75-86
